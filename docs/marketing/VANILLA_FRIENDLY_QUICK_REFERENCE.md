## Test Your Copy

1. Would my mom understand this? ✅/❌
2. Does this sound scary? ✅/❌
3. Would someone dismiss this? ✅/❌
4. Is this too abstract? ✅/❌
5. Does this focus on benefits? ✅/❌

---

## Key Principle

**Thesidia's core is cognition and pattern recognition—not gnostic philosophy.**

Focus on:
- ✅ Benefits (think clearer, see patterns)
- ✅ Simple language (everyone understands)
- ✅ Friendly tone (approachable, warm)
- ✅ Analogies (make abstract concrete)

Avoid:
- ❌ Philosophy (gnostic, Sophia)
- ❌ Triggering words (decode, expose, truth)
- ❌ Abstract concepts (consciousness, matrix)
- ❌ Scary language (hidden, erased, power)

---
