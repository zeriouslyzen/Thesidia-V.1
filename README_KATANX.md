# Katanx

**A social platform for practitioners of the arts of human development**

---

## Overview

Katanx is a social networking and discovery platform designed for individuals who practice, teach, and explore the full spectrum of human development arts. Built on the principle that human expression and growth manifest through diverse disciplines—from martial arts to visual arts, from movement to meditation—Katanx connects practitioners, teachers, and students across all domains of human development.

---

## The Arts of Human Development

Katanx is founded on the recognition that humans have created countless disciplines to express and develop the body, mind, spirit, and skill. These arts are organized into nine core categories:

### 1. Martial & Combative Arts (Body-Mind Discipline)

Focused on **movement, power, awareness, self-defense, and character**.

- Kung Fu, Shaolin Arts
- Qigong, Neigong
- Karate, Taekwondo
- Jeet Kune Do
- Budo systems (Asayama Ichiden Ryu, Aikido, etc.)
- Boxing, Muay Thai, Wrestling, Jiu-Jitsu
- Weapons arts (Kobudo, Kali, Fencing)
- Internal martial arts (Taiji, Bagua, Xingyi)

**Core Essence:** *Refinement of movement, spirit, discipline, and response.*

---

### 2. Movement Arts (Expression through Motion)

Centered on **flow, creativity, athleticism, and body awareness**.

- Dance (all styles)
- Parkour / Freerunning
- Acrobatics
- Gymnastics
- Capoeira
- Yoga (as movement, not just spiritual)
- Contemporary movement (Gaga, Contact Improv)
- Flow arts (poi, staff, hoops)

**Core Essence:** *The body as an instrument of expression, rhythm, and flow.*

---

### 3. Visual Arts (Expression through Form)

Expression through **shape, color, texture, space**.

- Drawing, Painting, Sculpture
- Calligraphy
- Photography
- Film & Animation
- Architecture
- Design (UX/UI, graphic design)
- Crafts & artisan skills (jewelry making, engraving)

**Core Essence:** *Projecting inner vision into the physical world.*

---

### 4. Internal/Spiritual Arts (Development of Spirit & Perception)

The **inner work**: awareness, consciousness, energy.

- Meditation (mindfulness, Zen, Taoist, yogic, etc.)
- Pranayama / Breathwork
- Qigong (internal systems)
- Yoga (classical paths)
- Shamanic or indigenous practices
- Ritual arts (traditional, cultural)
- Mystical or metaphysical arts

**Core Essence:** *Purifying perception and connecting to higher meaning.*

---

### 5. Performance Arts (Expression through Presence)

Focused on **emotions, storytelling, embodiment**.

- Theater
- Spoken word / Poetry
- Singing & Music
- Ritual performance
- Martial dance (e.g., Wushu performances)

**Core Essence:** *The art of transmitting energy, feeling, and story.*

---

### 6. Healing Arts (Restoration & Balance)

Focused on **health, energy, longevity, balance**.

- Traditional Chinese Medicine
- Acupuncture, acupressure
- Herbalism
- Massage, bodywork, Thai massage
- Modern physical therapy
- Energetic healing practices (Reiki, Qi-based methods)

**Core Essence:** *Returning the body-mind to its natural harmony.*

---

### 7. Intellectual Arts (Mind Development)

The arts of **thought**, pattern recognition, logic, and creativity.

- Philosophy
- Science
- Mathematics
- Language and linguistics
- Systems thinking
- Strategy (Sun Tzu, military strategy, game theory)

**Core Essence:** *Sharpening understanding and expanding awareness.*

---

### 8. Creative/Inventive Arts (Building New Realities)

The arts of **innovation**, engineering, and creation.

- Invention (ancient devices, astrolabes, sundials)
- Engineering
- Architecture
- Programming (as creative design)
- Alchemy (classical and metaphorical)
- Machine-building, mechanism design

**Core Essence:** *Turning imagination into functioning reality.*

---

### 9. Social/Leadership Arts (Human Interaction Mastery)

The arts that develop **communication, influence, harmony**.

- Rhetoric
- Diplomacy
- Teaching
- Leadership arts
- Community building
- Psychosocial development

**Core Essence:** *Mastery of connection, group energy, and shared purpose.*

---

## Platform Purpose

Katanx serves as a unified platform where practitioners across all nine categories of human development arts can:

### Connect & Discover
- **Find practitioners** across disciplines and locations
- **Discover teachers and studios** in your area or online
- **Connect with like-minded individuals** who share your interests
- **Explore new disciplines** through curated content and recommendations

### Share & Learn
- **Portfolio showcase**: Display your work, practice, and journey
- **KX Cuts**: Share insights, techniques, and moments of discovery
- **Origin stories**: Tell your personal journey in your chosen arts
- **Educational content**: Learn from practitioners and teachers

### Build Community
- **Circles**: Join or create communities around specific disciplines
- **Resonating**: Connect with content and people that resonate with your practice
- **Friends & Fans**: Build meaningful connections with fellow practitioners
- **Studio integration**: Link to studios, gyms, and training locations

### Express Identity
- **Multi-disciplinary profiles**: Showcase up to 2 disciplines per category
- **Role identification**: Identify as Teacher, Practitioner, Artist, Coach, etc.
- **Portfolio sections**: 
  - Reels of your practice/work
  - Origin story
  - Reading lists
  - Mind-body disciplines
  - Credentials and education
  - Services offered
  - Studios and locations

## Core Features

### Profile System
- **Rectangular avatars** (default, customizable)
- **Discipline chips**: Display your primary disciplines with category/subcategory
- **Portfolio sections**: Comprehensive showcase of your work and journey
- **Social links**: Connect Instagram, X (Twitter), Facebook, TikTok, LinkedIn
- **Metrics**: Friends, Fans, Resonating, Cuts

### Content Discovery
- **Stream**: Personalized feed of content from your network
- **KX Cuts**: Curated insights and discoveries from practitioners
- **Portfolio**: Browse practitioner portfolios and work
- **Dojo**: Training and educational content

### Navigation & Status
- **Status system**: Online, Offline, Away, Focused
- **Clean navigation**: Home, Stream, KX Cuts, Circles, Studio
- **Responsive design**: Mobile-first, works on all devices

### Design Philosophy
- **Inconsolata font**: Technical, readable, modern
- **Neon accents**: Light neon blue for titles, neon green for actions
- **Modular layout**: Clean, intuitive, efficient
- **E-reading friendly**: Optimized typography for reading origin stories and content

---

## Technical Architecture

Katanx is built as a modern web application with:

- **Frontend**: HTML, CSS, JavaScript (vanilla, no frameworks)
- **Backend**: Flask (Python) API server
- **Data**: JSON-based storage with mock profile system for development
- **Deployment**: Supports Vercel (frontend) + Railway/Render (backend)

See `README.md` for technical setup and `README_API.md` for API documentation.

## Philosophy

Katanx recognizes that human development is not limited to a single path. Whether you practice martial arts, create visual art, study philosophy, or teach healing arts, your journey is part of the larger tapestry of human expression and growth. The platform is designed to honor all paths while making it easier to find, connect with, and learn from practitioners across disciplines.

**The goal**: Create a space where the full spectrum of human development arts can coexist, cross-pollinate, and inspire one another.
