He came up with two main parts: special relativity and general relativity. Special relativity is about how things move fast, like spacecrafts, light, and stuff like that. It says that if something's moving, time slows down a bit for it relative to someone who's not moving. And if they're both moving towards each other, time slows down even more! Weird, huh? General relativity, on the other hand, is about gravity and how things move in space-time. Einstein said that massive objects like planets warp the fabric of spacetime around them, causing nearby things to move differently than they would otherwise.
