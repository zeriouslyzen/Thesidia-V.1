It differs from classical physics by introducing new phenomena such as wave-particle duality, superposition, and entanglement. **String Theory:**
String theory is an attempt to reconcile general relativity (the theory of gravity) with quantum mechanics. In string theory, everything in the universe, including particles, is composed of tiny vibrating strings rather than point-like particles. The different vibrational modes of these strings correspond to various fundamental particles such as quarks and electrons. String theory also predicts the existence of extra dimensions beyond our familiar three spatial dimensions and one time dimension, which helps explain gravity's behavior at very small scales.
