2. Ayurveda: This ancient Indian system emphasizes the balance between mind, body, and spirit through diet, lifestyle, herbs, yoga, and meditation.  - Mechanism Depth: Ayurvedic practices can help regulate the HPA axis, reducing stress levels and promoting better sleep. Regular practice of Yoga and meditation improves brainwave coherence and heart rate variability, enhancing focus, emotional balance, and overall well-being.

 - Mechanism Depth: MBSR helps reduce stress by focusing on mindfulness and body awareness, leading to lower cortisol levels, reduced inflammation markers, and enhanced cellular repair. CBT addresses negative thought patterns, promoting emotional balance and reducing anxiety. Biofeedback trains individuals to control physiological responses like heart rate and blood pressure through conscious thought.

By incorporating practices from all three traditions, you can create a holistic approach tailored to your needs for overall wellness.
