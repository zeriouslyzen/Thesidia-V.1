It's pretty much nature's way of turning sunlight into energy for everything that breathes. 🌱🌱🌱 Here's the basic rundown: 1. Absorption of sunlight by chlorophyll in leaves (the green pigment). 2. Splitting water molecules (H2O) into hydrogen (H+), oxygen (O2), and electrons (e-). 3. Using solar energy, those electrons power the conversion of carbon dioxide (CO2) and water (H2O) into glucose (C6H12O6) and oxygen (O2). 4. Excess oxygen is released as a byproduct 🍃🌱🌳 5. Plants feed on the glucose for energy, while photosynthetic organisms like algae can use it to reproduce. This process is essential for all life on Earth that doesn't eat other living things!
